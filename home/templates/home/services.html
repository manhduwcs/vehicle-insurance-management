Factors Affecting Insurance
Costs

Discover the main factors that influence how your vehicle insurance premium is calculated.

Value of Your Car

Higher car value means higher insurance cost because repairs and parts are more expensive.

Insurance Type

Comprehensive coverage provides wider protection but comes with a higher premium rate.

Coverage

The broader your coverage options, the more you’ll need to pay for insurance protection.

Car’s Age

Older vehicles often cost less to insure since their repair or replacement value is lower.

Bonus Features

Cars equipped with airbags and anti-theft systems usually qualify for lower premium rates.

Claim History

Drivers with fewer past claims are considered safer and receive better insurance discounts.
